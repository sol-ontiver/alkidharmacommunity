We're glad you're here. This site brings together news, schedules, and information about the Alki Dharma community — a buddhist meditation group in West Seattle.

## What you'll find

- **Blog** — announcements and reflections from the community
- **Events** — upcoming sits, classes, and gatherings

All content is published from plain Markdown files, listed under `content/posts` and `content/events`. To add a post, drop a new `.md` file in `content/posts` and rebuild the site.

## About the practice

Our doors are open to anyone interested in zazen and dharma study, whatever their level of experience. No need to sign up in advance — come as you are.
